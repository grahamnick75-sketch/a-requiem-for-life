# A Requiem for Life — Character Art Direction

Approved visual standard: modern-urban-fantasy cartoon character portraits; one complete full-body figure, clean black linework, simplified expressive anatomy, smooth cel shading, navy/dark-blue background with an electric-blue triangular grid, and a cyan halo outline. No text, character sheets, side panels, collages or multiple poses. Keep different characters distinct.
