Cal “Chain” Maddox

Medium Humanoid (Human), Neutral Evil · CR 4 (1,100 XP), provisional
Armor Class 15 (reinforced fighting leathers)
Hit Points 119 (14d8 + 56)
Speed 30 ft.
STR20 (+5)
DEX12 (+1)
CON18 (+4)
INT9 (−1)
WIS12 (+1)
CHA14 (+2)

Saving Throws Str +7, Con +6

Skills Athletics +9, Intimidation +6, Perception +3

Senses Passive Perception 13

Languages Common

Proficiency Bonus +2

Boss identity: A heavy-hitting arena bruiser who grapples foes and slams them into the fence. 3 Crushing Blows and 2 Adrenaline Surges are his limited resources.

Traits

Iron Stance. Cal has Advantage on Strength saving throws to avoid being pushed or knocked Prone.

Crushing Blow (3/Day). Once per turn when Cal hits with Heavy Fist, he can add 9 (2d8) Bludgeoning damage. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength save or fall Prone.

Actions

Multiattack. Cal makes two Heavy Fist attacks. He can replace one attack with Clinch or Fence Slam if eligible.

Heavy Fist. Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (1d10 + 5) Bludgeoning damage.

Clinch. One Large or smaller creature within 5 feet makes a DC 15 Strength or Dexterity save (its choice). On failure it is Grappled (escape DC 15). Cal can hold only one creature at a time this way.

Fence Slam. Requirement: Target grappled by Cal within 5 ft. of the fence. Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) Bludgeoning damage; target cannot take Reactions until the start of Cal’s next turn.

Bonus Actions

Adrenaline Surge (2/Day). Cal regains 11 (2d6 + 4) Hit Points; unusable at 0 HP.

Reactions

Counter-Clinch. When a creature within 5 ft. misses Cal with a melee attack, and he isn't holding another creature, he can force that attacker to make a DC 15 Strength or Dexterity save (attacker's choice). Failure: Grappled (escape DC 15).

DM Tactics

Early fight: Cal closes on a durable PC, grabs them, and drags them toward the fence; his enforcers exploit the opening.

Midfight: Slam a grappled foe into the fence. Use Crushing Blows to punish characters who remain in melee. The handler may release the blink dogs.

Bloodied: Spend Adrenaline Surges when needed and continue pressuring the party; Cal won't run in front of his spectators.

Counterplay: A grappled PC can use an action to escape with a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. Allies may intervene.

Fighting Ring — Hostile Crowd Lair Action

At initiative count 20 (losing ties), spectators throw debris at two PCs within 10 feet of the fence. Each makes a DC 13 Dexterity save. Failure: 1d4 Bludgeoning damage and Disadvantage on their next attack roll before the end of their next turn. A PC can spend an action and succeed on a DC 15 Charisma (Intimidation or Performance) check to suppress the crowd for one round. Hostile crowd activity ends when Cal falls.

Encounter: Six level-3 PCs; Cal has enforcers, a handler, and blink dogs. A PC can calm one blink dog using an action and a DC 15 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check (homebrew). Shipment clues are found in Cal's office or on his person. Portrait is embedded in this file and works offline. CR 4 is a design target pending playtest.
